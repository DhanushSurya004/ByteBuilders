Challenges Faced by Delivery Partners

Every delivery partner faces different challenges while completing an order.

Road Problems and Accidents

Delivery partners spend many hours travelling on busy roads. Unexpected road problems and accidents can sometimes affect their journey.

Customer Behaviour

Sometimes customers may become impatient when an order is delayed and may speak rudely to delivery partners.

However, delays can happen because of traffic, weather or restaurant delays.

Finding the Correct Address

Finding the correct address can sometimes be difficult, especially in unfamiliar locations.

Delivery Time Pressure

Delivery partners often feel pressure to reach customers on time while managing traffic and other unexpected situations.
