Support and Respect

Food delivery partners work hard every day to make our lives easier.

They also deserve respect, kindness and understanding from everyone.

Be Kind and Patient

Sometimes deliveries may be delayed because of traffic, weather or restaurant delays.

Instead of getting angry, we can try to be patient and speak respectfully.

A Small Thank You Matters

A simple "Thank You" can make a delivery partner feel appreciated.

Respecting their hard work can make their working day better.

Our Message

"Behind every delivery is a person working hard for you."

Let's choose kindness, patience and respect for the people behind every delivery.
