Daily Struggles of Delivery Partners

Food delivery partners work hard every day to deliver orders to customers on time.

They travel through busy roads and heavy traffic for many hours.

Weather Problems

Delivery partners may have to work during heavy rain and hot weather.

Even in difficult weather conditions, they try their best to complete deliveries.

Long Working Hours

They may work for long hours and travel to many different locations in a day.

This can make them physically tired, but they continue working to complete their deliveries.

A Small Thought

Every delivery takes effort. A little patience and kindness can make their day better.
